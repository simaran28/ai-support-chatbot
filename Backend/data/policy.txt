Company Leave Policy

Annual Leave:
Every employee gets 20 paid leaves per year.
Leaves can be carried forward to next year maximum 5 days.
Leave must be applied 3 days in advance.

Sick Leave:
Every employee gets 10 sick leaves per year.
Sick leave requires medical certificate if more than 2 days.

Work From Home Policy:
Employees can work from home 2 days per week.
Work from home must be approved by manager.
During work from home, employee must be available on calls.

Salary Policy:
Salary is credited on 1st of every month.
Salary slip is sent via email every month.
Increment is done every year in April month.
