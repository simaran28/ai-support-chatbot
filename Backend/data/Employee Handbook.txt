ABC Technologies Employee Handbook

Company Overview

ABC Technologies is a software development company founded in 2018. The company specializes in cloud computing, web applications, artificial intelligence, and enterprise software solutions. The headquarters is located in Bangalore, India.

Working Hours

Employees are expected to work from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday. Flexible work arrangements may be approved by managers. Employees working remotely must be available during core hours from 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

Leave Policy

Employees are entitled to:

* 20 paid leave days per year
* 10 sick leave days per year
* 12 public holidays

Unused paid leave can be carried forward up to a maximum of 10 days.

Performance Reviews

Performance reviews are conducted twice a year:

* Mid-Year Review in June
* Annual Review in December

Employees are evaluated on technical skills, teamwork, communication, leadership, and project contributions.

Code of Conduct

Employees must:

* Treat colleagues with respect
* Maintain confidentiality of company information
* Follow security guidelines
* Avoid workplace harassment

Benefits

Employees receive:

* Health insurance
* Life insurance
* Annual performance bonus
* Learning and certification reimbursement

Training Programs

ABC Technologies provides training in:

* Python Development
* Angular Development
* Cloud Computing
* Artificial Intelligence
* Cybersecurity

Conclusion

The company encourages continuous learning, collaboration, and innovation.
